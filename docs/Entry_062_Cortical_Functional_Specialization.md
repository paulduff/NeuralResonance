# Entry 062 - Cortical Functional Specialization

Date: 2026-08-05

## Purpose

Entry 061 made twelve missing cortical territories runnable and connected. This rung gives those services distinct local signal organization so their functional identities are implemented in computation rather than represented only by names and connectome labels.

## Specialized Kernels

- Visual association kernel: V3 preserves retinotopic form and depth organization; inferotemporal cortex separates feedforward feature, motion, thalamic-attention, semantic, and executive streams; fusiform cortex uses recurrent expertise channels.
- Auditory association kernel: preserves tonotopic channels while separating acoustic, semantic, voice-context, and language feedback streams.
- Somatosensory association kernel: preserves face/head, hand/arm, trunk, and leg/foot territories while allowing feedback and high-arousal bursts.
- Self-context kernel: separates body/agency, episodic memory, affect, multimodal semantics, and executive evidence for temporal pole, TPJ, and precuneus processing.
- Executive-control kernel: separates policy, conflict, valuation, basal-ganglia, and spatial-attention streams. Frontal eye fields retain a spatial map and emit attention-dependent bursts; midcingulate bursts on significant prediction error; medial prefrontal circuits use dopamine-gated bursts.

## Learning

The kernels do not install fixed answers or symbolic rules. They determine where biologically distinct input streams arrive and how output populations are addressed. Existing STDP, BCM, dopamine-modulated plasticity, synaptic tagging, dendritic integration, homeostasis, metabolism, and persistent synapse storage continue to determine what each population learns.

## Verification

Runtime tests now require:

- every new territory to select a purpose-specific kernel;
- visual, auditory, body, self-context, and executive input classes to map differently;
- body territories to remain non-overlapping;
- frontal eye field, midcingulate, and medial prefrontal output modes to respond to attention, error, and value signals;
- every mapping to be deterministic and remain within the circuit's neuron population.

## Boundary

These mechanisms establish differentiated computation, not proof of high-level cognition. Face expertise, social inference, self-model stability, object invariance, and adaptive gaze must still be demonstrated through embodied training and end-to-end behavioural benchmarks.
