The completed midpoint study `e503fd` passes its original numerical limits but
emits no nerve events. Its original head clip mostly inhibited the declared

The initial head has a canonical orthonormal frame, fixed origin, central
sensing point and unchanged downward gravity. It is deliberately distinct from
the rounded native head geometry. Each motion has its own declared session;
each resolution repeats the same motion/session in an isolated capture. The
physical clock explicitly names the prescribed diagnostic source. Positive yaw
is not assumed to be excitation: actual projected bundle motion and electrical
response determine the sign. The selected excursion is a diagnostic probe,
not a calibrated range for Avatar or proof of human sensory equivalence.

The source starts after the required initial head history, at neural time
1010 ms; it advances continuously to 1190 ms. The first stationary samples,
turn, deceleration and held tail all remain in the record. The skull adapter
derives its usual interval-average angular acceleration from completed sampled
velocities. The analytic acceleration is recorded for comparison and is not
substituted into an earlier physical interval.

No neuronal equation, receptor constant, firing threshold, loaded initial state,
material parameter or attachment polarity is changed. Earlier recorded/start-held
and midpoint entry points remain exact. The only existing source edit adds a
new explicit probe command; capture and analysis live in separate files.
