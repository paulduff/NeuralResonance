# Folded Archive Entry 104: Dyad Accepted Voice

## Decision

Dyad owns the final language-delivery path. Entity proposes candidate words,
DNNE accepts or defers the prompt-bound candidate from current neuronal state,
and a presentation adapter may voice only the exact accepted text.

## Boundary

- Speech requires Dyad language protocol v2 and an accepted DNNE review.
- Response and review session/turn identifiers must match.
- The neuronal language circuit must be observed, grounded, available, awake,
  and speech-authorized.
- The emitted text must be byte-for-byte equal to the reviewed candidate text.
- Deferred or modified candidates produce no audible output.

The adapter receives no raw spikes, prompt, memory, labels, or fallback phrase.
It cannot infer language and has no route back into DNNE. Voice selection and
waveform synthesis remain operating-system presentation concerns.

## Result

Entity can have a practical local voice without restoring host-authored speech
authority. This is an accepted-text presenter, not a neuronal vocal tract or a
claim that DNNE has learned speech acoustics.
