# Entry 143 - Overnight Contact Loop and Phasic Release

## Observation

The overnight predators-suspended run survived for 8.902 hours and completed
970,016 brain ticks at a mean 30.27 Hz without a tick failure. The transport
path was also stable: more than 6.2 million somatic frames produced one timeout,
and the control and editor error logs remained empty.

The run nevertheless settled into a long-lived shelter contact attractor:

- 80.24 percent of the run was classified as unstable;
- spinal withdrawal was active for 99.992 percent of the run;
- posterior chest contact occupied 86.9 percent of the run, including a
  25,670.8 second uninterrupted episode;
- posterior left-hand contact occupied 78.7 percent, including a 24,752.3
  second uninterrupted episode;
- stand, both coronal hip lanes, both sagittal ankle lanes, and the manipulator
  remained active for nearly the entire observation;
- several participating muscles reached complete fatigue and produced no
  useful force; and
- all 111 interaction attempts were out of reach.

The removed doorway lintel was successful: head contact lasted only about nine
seconds and did not support the body. The remaining failure was a chest-and-hand
brace against the wall or doorway corner.

## Interpretation

The body was receiving truthful pressure, fatigue, and negative-prediction
evidence, but the same unchanging mechanonociceptive input continuously renewed
the spinal flexor-withdrawal population. Withdrawal therefore ceased to be a
short protective event and became part of the motor pressure maintaining the
contact. The loop was:

`contact -> tonic withdrawal -> sustained motor pressure -> renewed contact`.

Tissue damage would make a prolonged harmful contact more truthful and should
remain a future consequence of excessive pressure, ischemic duration, or
trauma. It is not the immediate repair for this loop. Damage alone would add a
larger pain signal to a pathway already locked on and could intensify the same
motor attractor. The immediate repair must let the responsible motor population
release while retaining pain as continuous sensory evidence.

## Implemented neuronal repair

1. Ascending free-nerve-ending nociception remains tonic for sustained harmful
   pressure.
2. Its spinal collateral is now phasic. Contact onset, slip, rising force,
   damaging penetration, and severe load still recruit immediate local
   withdrawal. Unchanging static pressure adapts into separated pulses instead
   of a permanent flexor command.
3. Rising force during the adapted interval retriggers withdrawal immediately.
4. Sustained habenular aversive activity inhibits the action population that
   was temporally responsible for the costly outcome after its persistence
   interval. It does not specify an escape direction; another neuronal
   population must win independently.
5. A bilateral neuronal righting response during a large measured balance error
   releases unrelated voluntary locomotor and limb lanes while retaining stand
   recruitment.
6. A fresh local spinal withdrawal reflex keeps priority over that righting
   release, preserving immediate protection.
7. Existing muscle fatigue, group III/IV somatic distress, exact zero-relaxed
   activation, and physical force loss remain unchanged.

No scripted detachment, automatic turn, host-authored escape vector, symbolic
movement policy, or ML controller was introduced.
