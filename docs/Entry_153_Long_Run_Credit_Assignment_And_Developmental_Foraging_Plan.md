# Entry 153 - Long-Run Credit Assignment and Developmental Foraging Plan

Date: 2026-08-22

## Status

Implemented in code and focused verification on 2026-08-22. The stack remains
gracefully stopped. Structural action-topology changes require a fresh network;
the old experimental weights are not an acceptance artifact.

The long-duration biological acceptance ladder remains intentionally unclaimed.
Compilation and deterministic assays prove contracts and mechanisms, but only
fresh-weight live runs can show that useful embodied behavior develops.

## Mandate

The DNNE must remain neuronal. The host may model anatomy, physics, tissue,
metabolism, sensory transduction, conduction, and the consequences of physical
acts. It must not select a useful action, steer toward a target, grant success
for an intended behavior, or conceal neuronal failure with scripted movement.

The following boundaries remain non-negotiable:

- no ML policy, classifier, optimizer, or host-authored behavioral authority;
- no restoration of hidden root-translation reserves;
- no weakening of collision, tissue, pressure, withdrawal, or fatigue truth;
- no automatic host recovery from a poor pose merely to keep the run moving;
- no reward for a reset or other unearned restoration;
- no preservation requirement for experimental weights that have not passed
  an acceptance run.

## Gait and fatigue findings

During 8,427 seconds of gait-eligible time, double support occupied 6,057
seconds, or 71.9%. There were 884 alternating transitions but 1,499 repeated
same-side transitions. Both feet entered swing and cleared the floor, proving
that the plant can lift a foot, but left swing lasted as long as 17.40 seconds
and right swing 7.55 seconds.

This is not a simple lack of leg force. It is a failure of reciprocal phase
selection and release. At shutdown, bilateral iliopsoas, hamstrings, and
gluteus medius populations were fully fatigued. That fatigue is valid evidence:
the network sustained incompatible or unproductive recruitment for too long.
Removing the fatigue consequence would hide the control defect.

The motor record also shows tonic output. Left and right root motor channels
were active for about 91% of samples, the stand channel for 90.7%, manipulator
output for 96.7%, and most articulated joint channels for 55% to 72%. A real
relaxed zero state is therefore not winning neuronal competition often enough.

## Neural-state finding: false action authority

The retained snapshot set contains 3,905 observations. Forty evenly distributed
Striatum samples from tick 34,584 through tick 143,136 were examined in detail.
Across all of them:

- mean Striatum firing rate was 0 Hz;
- peak direct-pathway activation was 0;
- peak indirect-pathway activation was 0;
- peak motor-thalamic relay activation was 0;
- mean eligibility trace was about 0.9915, with a 0.9806 to 0.9983 range;
- learned corticostriatal strength remained near its minimum, about 0.057;
- the mean winning margin was only about 0.00234.

The rate values are collected directly from each model neuron's firing rate.
The action-channel projection code preserves channel identity through the
basal ganglia and Motor Thalamus. This is therefore not explained by a display
convention or by channel scrambling.

The decoder currently treats the presence of a Striatum snapshot as full
striatal circuit coverage. It can authorize a channel when total coverage is
at least 0.60, the score exceeds 0.01, the margin exceeds 0.0025, and output
inhibition is below 0.90. Cortical proposal, learned-strength, and positive
eligibility terms can satisfy that rule even when direct, indirect, and
thalamic activity are all zero.

This is the highest-confidence primary defect. Anatomical services are present,
but presence is being mistaken for functional participation. Cortical proposal
noise, saturated eligibility, and persistence can acquire host-side authority
without a completed cortico-basal-ganglia-thalamic selection loop.

## Credit-assignment finding

Positive teaching currently includes measured movement during motor-training
mode. The signal does not require displacement toward a resource, release from
an obstacle, a successful reach, reduced fatigue, or an earned homeostatic
restoration. Any motion can contribute appetitive VTA and SNc teaching.

This was originally a bootstrap aid, but the long run shows its failure mode:
shuffling, turning in place, leaning, and repeatedly moving limbs near a wall
can all be reinforced. No food or water success ever supplied the stronger
biological teaching event that was meant to replace this bridge.

Eligibility is also saturated while learned strength remains near its floor.
Eligibility is a temporal credit trace, not evidence that an action is valid.
Allowing it to contribute directly to action authority creates a circular
failure: nearly every channel stays eligible, thin proposal differences select
one, generic motion rewards the resulting movement, and no useful policy is
resolved.

## Interaction-semantics finding

The motor bridge derives `ManipulatorDrive` from the maximum magnitude of any
shoulder or elbow command. Peripersonal PPC activity can gate that value, but
there is no distinct grasp, hand closure, contact-maintenance, or release
population.

WorldSim treats each latched manipulator pulse as a physical interaction
attempt. Consequently, ordinary arm exploration generated 579 attempts even
though every physical target was more than 1.20 m away. This telemetry is not
showing repeated failed grasp decisions; it is showing that arm movement and
interaction intent are conflated.

## Observability finding

The long-run report is reliable for world, body, transport, contacts, gait, and
metabolic outcomes. Some persisted cross-structure neural diagnostics are not
yet reliable enough for gain tuning:

- only structures selected in the current scheduler tick are written into a
  persisted snapshot;
- snapshot persistence occurs every sixth tick, while scheduler selection is
  deterministic;
- 34 structure types, including LHA and Nucleus Accumbens, never appeared in
  the retained persisted sample even though their services were online;
- top-level persisted neuromodulator state and reward-prediction error are
  currently initialized as zero placeholders;
- cross-structure enriched fields can read zero when the source and target were
  simply not sampled on the same tick.

This is a sampling alias and composition problem. It does not prove that every
missing structure failed to tick. It does mean the next repair cannot safely
begin by tuning LHA, Nucleus Accumbens, VTA, or scheduler gains from the current
composite log alone.

## Root-cause hierarchy

### Confirmed primary causes

1. **Action authority is not functionally gated.** Silent Striatum and absent
   thalamic relay can still produce an authorized voluntary action.
2. **The bootstrap reward is non-specific.** Motion receives positive teaching
   without useful consequence, while no earned resource outcome has occurred.
3. **Interaction intent is not anatomically distinct.** Arm movement is treated
   as manipulation, making every interaction statistic misleading and denying
   the network a learnable reach-contact-release sequence.
4. **Gait phases do not reciprocally resolve.** Repeated same-side transitions,
   long swing phases, and prolonged double support drive valid muscle fatigue.
5. **Protective balance and voluntary motor populations chatter.** Righting,
   posture, and gait repeatedly compete instead of handing authority over.

### High-priority uncertainties

1. Homeostatic and reward populations may run at too low or too irregular a
   cadence relative to the fast motor loop.
2. Hunger and thirst may reach LHA as arousal but fail to form a temporally
   aligned LHA/NAc/PFC action bias.
3. Striatal silence may arise from intrinsic excitability, corticostriatal
   projection strength, inhibition, timing, or a combination of them.
4. Current persistence cannot distinguish useful continuation from a tonic
   attractor because functional basal-ganglia evidence is not required.

These are not to be solved by assumption. Entry 153A exists to make them
measurable.

## Repair plan

Each rung is independently testable and should be committed only after its
acceptance gates pass. A failed rung stops progression; later tuning must not
be used to mask an earlier failure.

### Entry 153A - Make neural measurement truthful

1. Maintain a rolling last-known snapshot for every bilateral structure with
   source tick, age, cadence, and freshness. Persist the full set or explicitly
   persist a bounded-age aggregate instead of only the current scheduler slice.
2. Break the deterministic alias between scheduler cadence and snapshot cadence.
3. Persist actual local neuromodulator state and reward-prediction evidence.
   Remove zero placeholders from the authoritative record.
4. Keep raw local diagnostics separate from cross-structure enriched views.
   Never overwrite a structure's source identity with an aggregate carrier.
5. Record, for every action channel over time: proposal, D1/direct activity,
   D2/indirect activity, STN suppression, GPi/SNr output, Motor Thalamus relay,
   eligibility, learned strength, score, margin, persistence, inhibition,
   selection, and the exact reason authority was granted or denied.
6. Record teaching-event counts and magnitudes by physical cause: food, water,
   tissue recovery, balance recovery, movement bootstrap, ineffective force,
   fatigue, injury, and death.
7. Record death timestamp and cause, nearest resource distance, hand-to-target
   distance, interaction phase, and no-progress intervals.

Acceptance:

- all 119 structure types appear with bounded and explicit age;
- sampled raw and persisted firing rates agree;
- neuromodulator and teaching fields are not placeholders;
- a complete causal trace can explain every authorized motor channel;
- metabolic deaths are labelled separately from trauma and predation.

### Entry 153B - Restore basal-ganglia authority

1. Replace snapshot-presence coverage with functional evidence. A voluntary
   action must require channel-specific cortical proposal, active striatal
   competition, output-nucleus disinhibition, and Motor Thalamus relay.
2. A silent Striatum must produce no voluntary descending command. Eligibility
   or persistence alone must never authorize an action.
3. Remove eligibility from the immediate action score. Preserve it solely as a
   bounded temporal trace used when a later phasic teaching signal modifies
   corticostriatal synapses.
4. Diagnose and repair striatal excitability and D1/D2 balance at the neuronal
   level. Verify channel-preserving corticostriatal, pallidal, nigral, STN, and
   thalamic projections with evoked-spike tests.
5. Represent behavioral quiescence neuronally. Either a dedicated release/rest
   population or tonic indirect/output-nucleus inhibition must be able to win;
   the host must not manufacture rest by muting a valid neuronal command.
6. Bound persistence by continuing functional loop evidence and terminate it
   when the responsible population loses thalamic relay or receives phasic
   aversive teaching.

Acceptance:

- silent Striatum means zero voluntary action in unit and live integration tests;
- saturated eligibility without D1/D2 firing cannot select a channel;
- every authorized command has non-zero channel-specific relay evidence;
- genuine quiescent periods occur with muscle recruitment near zero;
- a forced cortical proposal can be stopped by indirect/hyperdirect activity.

### Entry 153C - Separate reach, grasp, contact, and release

1. Add neuronal hand and grasp populations rather than deriving manipulation
   from shoulder and elbow magnitude.
2. Keep shoulder, elbow, wrist, and hand movement as articulation. Only a
   neuronal grasp/contact population may open an interaction cycle.
3. Require anatomically appropriate hand position, near-space sensory evidence,
   an unobstructed reach, and hand contact before a physical resource can be
   consumed or held.
4. Transduce hand load, slip, pressure, and successful contact back through the
   existing somatic pathways. Release must be a neuronal outcome, including
   fatigue- or pain-driven loss of grip.
5. Report reach, contact, grasp, hold, release, miss, occlusion, and out-of-reach
   as separate phases.

Acceptance:

- free arm movement produces no interaction attempt;
- an explicit grasp population without reach produces an out-of-reach result;
- hand contact plus grasp can consume a reachable food item;
- contact and release are visible in sided somatic telemetry.

### Entry 153D - Establish a biological developmental curriculum

The environment may be arranged developmentally; the avatar may not be steered.

1. Remove the generic `measuredMotion` contribution from global appetitive
   teaching after the basal-ganglia gate is repaired.
2. Keep phasic positive teaching for physically earned reductions in hunger,
   thirst, tissue distress, or other biological deficit.
3. Keep local cerebellar and spinal error learning separate from global reward.
   Balance correction must not make every coincident action appetitive.
4. Begin with predators suspended, flat traversable ground, and one visible food
   inside reachable hand space. This changes the environment only; it does not
   tell the network which motor channel to choose.
5. Progress only after earned success:
   - stage 1: contact and consume food already within reach;
   - stage 2: reach food 2 to 4 m away on flat ground;
   - stage 3: reach food at the normal 12 m minimum radius;
   - stage 4: navigate graded terrain and shelter geometry;
   - stage 5: restore non-predatory complexity, then controlled predators.
6. Start each developmental experiment from a fresh network unless the prior
   stage's weights have passed its acceptance gates.

Acceptance:

- at least one food restoration is physically earned before metabolic death in
  stage 1;
- the phasic teaching trace follows consumption, not mere motion or respawn;
- learned corticostriatal strength changes only on eligible causal channels;
- success remains possible when host logs are disabled, proving no display or
  supervisor is supplying behavior.

### Entry 153E - Repair the neuronal gait handoff

1. Verify bilateral spinal half-centre inhibition and refractory phase timing.
2. Make plantar load and proprioceptive limb phase entrain stance-to-swing and
   contralateral swing-to-stance transitions.
3. Prevent a new same-side swing until contralateral support and phase evidence
   satisfy the neuronal transition.
4. Abort an overlong swing through neuronal sensory feedback and inhibitory
   competition, not host placement of the foot.
5. Feed measured muscle fatigue into recruitment capacity and sensory distress,
   preserving the current zero-reserve physical plant.
6. Keep ankle, hip abduction/adduction, trunk rotation, and vestibular feedback
   available to balance; do not collapse gait back to root translation.

Acceptance on a 30-minute flat-world run:

- at least 70% of gait transitions are alternating;
- repeated same-side transitions remain below 30%;
- no swing phase exceeds 2 seconds without a recorded physical obstruction;
- double support is no longer the dominant eligible gait state;
- locomotion does not require a hidden translation reserve;
- bilateral prime movers do not remain at complete fatigue while progress is
  absent.

### Entry 153F - Coordinate balance, righting, and voluntary action

1. Retain full 360-degree centre-of-mass and support-polygon physics.
2. Preserve acceptable biological instability. Stable does not mean perfectly
   centred; it means the projected centre of mass and momentum remain within a
   learned recoverable envelope.
3. Give protective vestibular/cerebellar/righting populations priority only
   when the dynamic envelope is genuinely exceeded.
4. Add neuronal hysteresis and refractory handoff so falling, righting, posture,
   and gait cannot alternate at control-tick frequency.
5. End righting authority after support is physically recovered and allow the
   voluntary loop to resume through fresh basal-ganglia selection.

Acceptance:

- falling/righting chatter drops below 10 entries per minute in an unobstructed
  flat-world run;
- genuine lateral and sagittal instability still causes a fall;
- physical support or counterbalance can recover without host pose correction;
- stable percentage is interpreted alongside progress and transition rate, not
  used as a stand-alone success score.

### Entry 153G - Close the homeostatic motivational loop

This rung follows truthful cadence measurement; it is not a preselected gain
change.

1. Measure effective tick cadence and spike propagation through Visceral
   Afferents, NTS, Arcuate, LHA/VMH, Nucleus Accumbens, VTA/SNc, PFC/OFC/ACC,
   Striatum, and Motor Thalamus.
2. If timing is insufficient, introduce a bounded-cadence homeostatic teaching
   lane in the scheduler. It must deliver neuronal spikes, not host action bias.
3. Confirm that hunger and thirst alter neuronal salience and action eligibility
   while leaving the final motor choice to basal-ganglia competition.
4. Confirm that actual deficit reduction produces phasic dopamine and updates
   only temporally eligible corticostriatal synapses.

Acceptance:

- every link in the motivational loop has measurable, age-bounded activity;
- hunger changes neuronal competition without directly selecting a motor lane;
- consumption creates a phasic signal and durable but bounded synaptic change;
- absence of a resource does not create endless tonic movement reward.

### Entry 153H - Acceptance ladder

Run the following in order, starting from fresh weights after structural changes:

1. **Circuit assay:** evoked action-channel spikes through cortex, Striatum,
   pallidum/nigra, Motor Thalamus, and spinal output.
2. **Quiescence assay:** no target and no acute deficit; verify real neuronal
   release and low muscle activation.
3. **Reach assay:** visible food inside hand range; verify reach, grasp, contact,
   consumption, and earned reward.
4. **Flat locomotion assay:** food 2 to 4 m away; verify reciprocal gait, balance,
   progress, fatigue release, and successful interaction.
5. **Normal-radius assay:** food at 12 m or more; verify spatially sustained
   behavior without generic motion reward.
6. **Three-hour world assay:** graded terrain and shelters, predators still
   suspended. Require transport health, metabolic survival through earned
   intake, useful exploration, and no tonic motor attractor.
7. **Predator assay:** only after the prior stages pass, restore controlled
   threats and assess fear, withdrawal, memory, and recovery without corrupting
   the developmental locomotor weights.

## Decision

The avatar does not need more force, less fatigue, looser collision, or another
broad balance tolerance. Its physical body is now revealing the real neural
problem: motor output is being accepted without completed basal-ganglia
selection, and the available teaching signal rewards movement before the body
has ever achieved a biologically useful outcome.

The first implementation is therefore Entry 153A. Only after the telemetry can
prove which neuronal populations fired, which teaching event occurred, and why
a channel acquired authority should Entry 153B alter the selection circuit.
This order is slower than a quick fix, but it protects the scientific value of
the DNNE and gives every later change a falsifiable acceptance gate.

## Implementation ledger

### 153A - Truthful telemetry

Implemented age-bounded structure activity, complete action-authority evidence,
teaching-signal cause attribution, physical-frame cadence, and homeostatic-lane
cadence counters. Snapshot diagnostics now distinguish observed activity from
fresh causal evidence and report why an action did or did not receive authority.

### 153B - Strict neuronal action authority

Voluntary output now requires a channel-specific cortical proposal, active
striatal competition, output-nucleus disinhibition, and fresh Motor Thalamus
relay. Eligibility is retained only as a bounded learning trace. Silent
Striatum, saturated eligibility without loop activity, stale relay, and output
nucleus suppression cannot authorize voluntary movement. The topology contains
38 explicit action channels and uses the fresh namespace
`synapses-action38-hand-v1`.

### 153C - Physical reach and grasp

Added sided neuronal hand-close/open populations, physical hand aperture, grip
force, fatigue and slip, articulated hand colliders, and a staged reach-contact-
grasp-hold-release plant. Arm motion alone cannot open an interaction cycle.
Consumption requires reachable, unobstructed hand contact and sustained grasp;
load, pressure, slip, fatigue, contact, and release return through somatic and
proprioceptive pathways.

### 153D - Developmental foraging

Added explicit `HandSpace`, `NearFlat`, `NormalDistance`, `Terrain`, and
`Ecology` world stages. The stages arrange environment complexity and resource
distance without steering the avatar. Predators are unavailable before the
ecology stage. Generic measured motion and balance correction no longer produce
global appetitive teaching; biological deficit reduction remains the earned
positive event.

### 153E - Neuronal spinal gait

Replaced the host sinusoidal gait phase clock with reciprocal neuronal spinal
half-centres. Plantar load, proprioceptive phase evidence, contralateral support,
refractory timing, swing timeout inhibition, and muscular fatigue now determine
phase recruitment. The obsolete host phase-advance helper and its tests were
removed so there is no second hidden gait authority.

### 153F - Physical righting and handoff

Righting now uses body mass, gravity, measured inertia, restoring torque,
angular velocity, and support physics rather than direct angle correction or
pose snapping. A hysteretic neuronal righting latch suppresses stale voluntary
commands during recovery, requires sustained physical recovery, emits one quiet
handoff frame, and then accepts only a fresh basal-ganglia selection.

### 153G - Bounded homeostatic cadence

Fast proprioceptive, somatic, and vestibular afference continues at physical
frame cadence. Visceral and phasic teaching populations use a 250 ms neuronal
cadence lane. Peak earned teaching events are buffered between dispatches so
they cannot be lost, duplicated, or converted into host action bias. Telemetry
reports dispatched and buffered frames separately.

### 153H - Verification state

Deterministic circuit, quiescence, reach, developmental-world, spinal-gait,
righting, and cadence tests are implemented. The Blazor editor exposes the
development stage, sided hand aperture, physical grip phase and force, hand
contact/hold/release sequence, misses, and fatigue releases.

The 30-minute reciprocal-gait gate, normal-radius fresh-weight gate, three-hour
world gate, and controlled-predator gate remain pending live experiments. They
must be run in that order from fresh weights; none may be inferred from unit
tests or from the pre-Entry-153 checkpoint.
