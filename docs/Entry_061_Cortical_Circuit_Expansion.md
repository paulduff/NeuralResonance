# Entry 061 - Cortical Circuit Expansion

Date: 2026-08-05

## Purpose

The editor's neutral cortical mantle exposed a real architectural gap: DNNE represented only 23 explicit cortical services, leaving major sensory-association, temporal-recognition, medial, and executive territories absent from both runtime and connectome.

This entry records the first deliberate cortical expansion. It does not claim that DNNE now models every cytoarchitectonic area. Each added coloured territory is a runnable bilateral structure service with an explicit dynamics profile, registry endpoint, inbound and outbound connectivity, and a fitted anatomical parcel.

## Added Circuits

- V3 for intermediate extrastriate form and depth integration.
- Auditory association cortex for complex sound, voice, rhythm, and learned auditory objects.
- Secondary somatosensory cortex for bilateral touch, texture, pain, and body-state integration.
- Inferotemporal cortex and fusiform gyrus for invariant object and learned visual-category recognition.
- Temporal pole for person, social, affective, and multimodal semantic context.
- Temporoparietal junction for attentional reorientation, agency, perspective, and event boundaries.
- Precuneus for self-location, imagery, scene construction, and episodic context.
- Midcingulate cortex for effort, pain, uncertainty, and motivated action control.
- Dorsomedial and ventromedial prefrontal cortex for policy/social inference and value/affective integration.
- Frontal eye fields for executive visual orientation and saccade planning.

## Runtime Contract

Every circuit has:

- a `StructureId` protocol identity;
- a dedicated structure-host project using the shared runtime;
- a local service endpoint and bilateral-hosting compatibility;
- explicit neuron, plasticity, and default-target profiles;
- at least one incoming and outgoing connectome route;
- a circuit-mode cortical territory in the WPF editor.

The generic cortical mantle remains visible in anatomy mode. It represents biological cortex and preserves the complete outer shape, while circuit mode distinguishes currently implemented computational territories from cortex not yet separated into services.

## Next Expansion

The next tranche should split the remaining broad umbrella services only when the additional modules gain distinct dynamics and routes. Candidates include superior and inferior parietal lobules, anterior insula, gustatory cortex, orbitomedial subdivisions, lateral occipital cortex, visual word-form circuitry, parahippocampal scene-selective cortex, and explicit left/right asymmetry for strongly lateralised functions.

## Boundary

More named parcels do not by themselves make DNNE more intelligent. New modules must earn their place through differentiated computation, embodied inputs, learnable connectivity, and reproducible behavioural tests.
